## 1. Executive Summary

Career Lab HQ is an AI-powered career operating system that helps professionals get promoted, navigate workplace challenges, lead more effectively, and build measurable career progress.

Unlike generic AI chat tools or traditional career coaching, Career Lab HQ combines proprietary diagnostic frameworks, structured scoring, and a persistent AI advisor to give professionals a clear picture of where they stand, what is blocking them, and what to do next — every single week.

The system delivers four things no AI chatbot or coaching service currently provides together:

1. A scored diagnosis of career readiness across defined dimensions
2. A personalized 90-day action plan built from that diagnosis
3. A weekly operating rhythm that tracks progress and updates recommendations
4. A human escalation layer for high-stakes career moments

**Current stage:** Active beta validation with an MVP server deployed and the Flowise platform in development.

**Immediate goal:** 10-25 paying beta users at $99-$299 to validate willingness to pay before scaling the full platform.

**Revenue target:** $10,000 MRR within 12 months.

## 3. The Problem

### For the Advancement Seeker

Millions of professionals are stuck in roles they have outgrown, waiting for a promotion that never comes. They do not know what is actually blocking them. They work hard, assume results are enough, and are confused and demoralized when they are passed over. They get vague feedback like "not ready yet" or "keep doing what you are doing" with no actionable path forward.

### For the Workplace Navigator

Every year, millions of professionals face workplace crises they are completely unprepared for. A surprise PIP. A manager who is undermining them. A political environment they do not know how to navigate. A conflict with a colleague that is damaging their reputation. They either suffer in silence, react badly, or pay a lawyer when all they needed was a clear framework and a trusted advisor to tell them what to do.

### For the Career Starter

New workers and early-career professionals enter the workforce without a manual. No one teaches them how to manage up, how to build a professional reputation, how to handle feedback, how to advocate for themselves, or how to navigate office dynamics. They make avoidable mistakes that set back their careers by years.

### The Common Thread

All three groups share the same core problem: **they lack a structured system that tells them where they stand and what to do next.** They are operating on instinct, hope, and generic advice — while the people around them who advance quickly have coaches, mentors, sponsors, and playbooks they can draw on.

Career Lab HQ gives everyone access to that system.

## 4. The Solution

### The Career Operating System

Career Lab HQ delivers a structured operating system across three tracks:

**GROW** — Where am I going?
Assessments, readiness scores, promotion strategy, leadership development, evidence vaults, and career blueprints for professionals focused on advancement.

**NAVIGATE** — How do I handle this?
Situation diagnostics, PIP response plans, manager navigation frameworks, workplace politics playbooks, and exit strategy builders for professionals dealing with active workplace challenges.

**LAUNCH** — Where do I start?
First-90-days blueprints, workplace fundamentals, manager relationship starters, reputation builders, and career change onboarding guides for new and early-career professionals.

### Why Users Pay Instead of Using ChatGPT

ChatGPT gives advice. Career Lab HQ provides a system.

Users pay for Career Lab HQ because it does four things ChatGPT cannot:

1. Diagnoses their career situation with a consistent, proprietary framework
2. Scores their readiness and tracks progress over time
3. Converts the diagnosis into a 90-day operating plan
4. Tells them exactly what to do next every single week

The proprietary frameworks — the Career Health Score, Promotion Readiness Score, Leadership Readiness Score, New Manager Readiness Score, and Workplace Intelligence diagnostic — are the product moat. AI is the delivery mechanism, not the product itself.

## 7. Assessment Framework

### Universal Response Scale

All scored questions use a 1-5 scale:

- 1 = Strongly disagree or not true
- 2 = Slightly true
- 3 = Somewhat true
- 4 = Mostly true
- 5 = Strongly true

Normalization formula: `component_score = ((average_response - 1) / 4) * 100`

Overall score: `overall_score = sum(component_score × component_weight)`

## 8. Scoring Engine

### Report Output

Every assessment produces the following output:

```
{
  overallScore: number (0-100),
  band: string,
  bandMeaning: string,
  componentScores: { [componentName]: number },
  strengths: [{ key, label, score }] (top 3),
  blockers: [{ key, label, score }] (bottom 3),
  nextActions: [string] (3 specific, personalized actions),
  blueprint: {
    outcome: string,
    day30: string,
    day60: string,
    day90: string,
    successMetric: string
  },
  escalationTrigger: string
}
```

### Human Escalation Triggers

The AI handles 80-95% of interactions. Escalate to human review when:

- Promotion score is 70+ and user is preparing a real promotion conversation
- User has salary negotiation within 30 days
- User reports a PIP, performance concern, or serious manager conflict
- User has a repeated blocker for 2 or more check-ins
- User's confidence score drops by 2 or more points
- User requests review of a promotion packet, self-review, or manager script
- AI detects legal, harassment, discrimination, or crisis-adjacent language

**Escalation risk levels:**

| Level | Type | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Low | Tactical script review | Async review, 1 business day |
| Medium | Promotion, interview, or manager conversation | Async review + brief call option |
| High | Salary negotiation, performance concern, conflict | Priority review + strategy call |
| Critical | Legal, harassment, discrimination, safety | Immediate boundary language + professional referral |

## 9. AI Knowledge System

### Purpose

Build the company's defensible moat by turning founder expertise, coaching patterns, frameworks, templates, and user outcomes into a continuously improving Digital Career Strategist.

The AI behaves like an experienced HR Business Partner, leadership coach, promotion strategist, talent advisor, and career coach. It asks follow-up questions, challenges assumptions, identifies blind spots, recommends actions, and tracks progress.

### Knowledge Domains

Career clarity, promotion strategy, leadership development, new manager readiness, interview preparation, workplace influence, manager conversations, performance reviews, salary negotiation, career transition, layoff recovery, personal branding, PIP response, workplace navigation, conflict resolution.

### The AI Must Not

- Claim to guarantee promotions, jobs, or compensation outcomes
- Give legal advice
- Encourage reckless or retaliatory workplace behavior
- Replace human review for high-risk issues
- Generate generic motivational content without specificity

### User Memory Architecture

The AI maintains persistent memory across six categories:

1. **Goal memory** — primary goal, target role, timeline, motivation, constraints
2. **Assessment history** — scores, trends, interpretations over time
3. **Blueprint history** — milestones, action plan versions, progress
4. **Weekly check-in history** — completed actions, blockers, confidence, momentum
5. **Evidence vault** — accomplishments, metrics, recognition, stakeholder feedback
6. **Conversation history** — recommendations given, scripts generated, decisions made

### Retrieval Logic

Retrieve the minimum relevant context for the current user intent. Do not flood responses with generic advice. Personalize based on career stage, target outcome, score weaknesses, confidence level, and action completion pattern.

### Founder Knowledge Capture Rhythm

After every coaching session or user insight:
- Capture problem type
- Capture useful question asked
- Capture recommended action
- Capture blind spot identified
- Add reusable insight to knowledge backlog

Weekly:
- Review top user blockers
- Convert repeated patterns into playbooks
- Retire weak recommendations

- Row Level Security on all user-owned data

- Coaches only access assigned clients
- Admin actions logged in audit table
- No service-role keys exposed to browser
- Stripe and Cal.com webhook signatures validated
- AI endpoints rate-limited
- No legal, medical, or financial guarantees in AI output

### Interview Guide

**Before purchase:**
1. What career outcome are you trying to create?
2. What have you already tried?
3. Why does this matter right now?
4. What would make this worth paying for?
5. What would make you choose this over ChatGPT?

1. What part of the scorecard felt most accurate?
2. What surprised you?
3. What felt generic or not useful?

5. Would you pay to continue weekly tracking?

1. Did the system help you act?
2. Was the next action clear?
3. What blocked you?

- 80% complete the assessment

**Outcomes:**
- 70% report improved clarity after the diagnostic

### Failure Criteria — Stop or Pivot If

- Users will not pay for the diagnostic
- Users say ChatGPT is sufficient for their needs
